Settings

Protect local music

GhoulMusic can ask iOS to treat its local database as persistent storage. This helps protect it from automatic cleanup, but removing GhoulMusic from your Home Screen can still erase local app data.

Backup to iCloud / Files

The backup saves playlists, custom playlist photos, likes, play counts, recent-play history, and song organization. It does not duplicate your audio files.

Recommended recovery order

After reinstalling GhoulMusic: first import your songs again from iCloud, then restore your GhoulMusic backup. The app will match the re-imported tracks back to your playlists and likes.
